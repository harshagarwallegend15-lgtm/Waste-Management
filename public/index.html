Features

Accountability at every step

Every collection, report and problem is tied to a photo, a location and a timestamp — so good work is provable and rewards are fair.

📷

Photo verification

AI and computer vision compare before/after photos to confirm a doorstep collection really happened.

📍

GPS + time proof

Location and timestamps are attached to every photo, so no claim can slip through without evidence.

⭐

Rewards & leaderboards

Residents and collectors earn points for verified work, with live leaderboards that make progress visible.

🏆

Community challenges

Societies race toward shared targets. Hit them and every resident of the society gets a bonus.

🚨

Dumping reports

Spot irresponsible dumping? Report it with a photo and location — verified reports earn points.

🏘️

Society problem board

Post streetlight, drainage and cleanliness issues. Higher-scoring societies get priority attention.

How it works

From doorstep to verified collection

Five simple steps connect residents, collectors and the municipality in one accountable loop.

1

Request

The resident photographs their waste. GPS and time are recorded automatically.

2

Route

Collectors in the area see the pending request and the exact doorstep location.

3

Collect

The collector arrives, picks up the waste and uploads a fresh after-photo.

4

Verify

Computer vision compares photos and locations. Only a verified match earns points.

5

Reward & improve

Points, leaderboards and reports feed back to the community and the municipality.
