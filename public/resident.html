How verification works

  1. You capture your waste with GPS + timestamp.
  2. A collector in your area sees your request and location.
  3. After collecting, the collector uploads an after-photo.
  4. Computer vision + AI compare the two photos & locations.
  5. Only on verified match you earn +20 points.
